🐦 Looking for beta testers for PixPacket!

PixPacket is a pixel art & animation app I'm building for Android, designed to be easy to use with your fingers.

What you can do:
✏️ 15 tools: pencil, fill, shapes, dither, gradient, selections & more
🎞️ Layers, frames, onion skin and a live animation preview
🎨 Unlimited custom palettes + PICO-8, NES, Endesga 32 and more
📁 Folders, autosave and a Recently deleted bin
📤 Export GIF, PNG, sprite sheets, .aseprite and .piskel
🌗 Light & dark mode, landscape layout, left-handed mode

Want early access? Comment or DM me the email you use on Google Play and I'll add you to the closed test. All feedback (and bug reports!) is welcome 🙏

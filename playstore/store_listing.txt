APP NAME (max 30)
PixPacket

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Pixel art & animation made for your fingers: layers, frames and GIF export.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
PixPacket is a pixel art and animation editor built for your phone. Every tool, panel and gesture was designed for touch, so you can sketch sprites, paint tiles and animate characters comfortably anywhere.

DRAW WITH YOUR FINGERS
- One finger draws, two fingers pinch to zoom and pan
- Two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo
- Long-press anywhere to pick a color
- Precision cursor: aim like a trackpad, then hold a button to draw exactly where you want
- Full-screen mode hides everything but your art

15 TOOLS
Pencil with pixel-perfect lines, eraser, fill (contiguous or replace everywhere), line, rectangle, ellipse, color picker, lighten/darken shading, dither, spray, dithered gradient, move, rectangle select, lasso and magic wand. Mirror drawing horizontally and vertically, and pick any brush size.

SELECTIONS
Copy, cut, paste, flip, rotate, outline, fill or erase just the area you selected. Drawing tools stay inside the selection.

LAYERS AND ANIMATION
- Layers with opacity, lock, hide, solo, merge and rename
- Frames you can add, duplicate, reorder and reverse
- Onion skin, adjustable speed (1-30 fps) and a live animation preview
- Tile preview to check repeating patterns

COLORS AND PALETTES
- Make as many palettes as you like, with as many colors as you like
- Built-in palettes: PICO-8, DB16, Endesga 32, Game Boy, NES, Grayscale and more
- Build a palette from the colors in your sprite
- Color mixer with hue, saturation, brightness, transparency and hex codes

CANVAS AND GRID
Resize or scale the canvas, crop to a selection, trim empty borders, flip and rotate. Set your own grid: pixel lines, tile size, offset and line color.

OPEN AND EXPORT
- Export PNG, animated GIF, sprite sheets and ZIPs of PNG frames, scaled up to 32x
- Open and save .aseprite and .piskel files, keeping layers and frames
- Import images and GIFs, or slice a sprite sheet into frames
- Save to your gallery, to any folder, or share straight to other apps

STAY ORGANIZED
Folders for your sprites, automatic saving, and a Recently deleted bin that keeps deleted art for 30 days.

MADE FOR YOU
Light and dark themes, accent colors, a left-handed layout, a two-thumb landscape layout and adjustable gestures.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. PixPacket doesn't use the internet, and your art stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

PixPacket is in active development. Feedback and bug reports are very welcome on GitHub.

PixPacket Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · App: PixPacket (Android) · Developer: KimmelDevs

In short: PixPacket does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Your drawings stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information we collect

None. PixPacket has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

  • Your drawings: saved in the app's private storage on your phone so you can keep working on them. Deleted drawings stay in "Recently deleted" inside the app for 30 days, then are removed.
  • Your settings and palettes: saved on your phone (for example theme, accent color, and your custom palettes).

This data never leaves your device through PixPacket. Uninstalling the app removes it. Android's own device backup may include app data if you have backup turned on; that is handled by Google's backup service under your Google account settings, not by PixPacket.

Files you import or export

  • Import: PixPacket only reads files that you pick yourself in the system file or photo picker.
  • Export: when you save an image or project, it is written where you choose: your Pictures/PixPacket folder, a location you pick in the Files app, or an app you select in the share sheet. After that, the file is handled by that location or app, under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): used to save exported images to your Pictures folder. Newer Android versions save exports without this permission.

Children

PixPacket does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Beta testing

If you join the closed beta, you give the developer the email address linked to your Google Play account so it can be added to the test list in Google Play Console. That email is used only to give you access to the test and is removed from the list if you ask. Google Play handles test distribution under Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/KimmelDevs/PixPacket/issues.
